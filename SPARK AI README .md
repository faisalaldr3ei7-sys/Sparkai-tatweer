## What SparkAI Does

SparkAI is an AI-powered market intelligence tool that gives any entrepreneur — a farmer, a young founder, a woman running a home business — instant access to the kind of insight that used to cost thousands of dirhams and weeks of waiting.

You type a question. You get an answer. In seconds. In Arabic or English. On any phone.

**Real questions real entrepreneurs ask:**
- *"Is there demand for my product in my area?"*
- *"What do people in my community actually need?"*
- *"How do I know if my idea can make money?"*
- *"Where should I focus — and what should I avoid?"*

No consultants. No forms. No waiting. No cost.
